# New hockey staff roles brief

## Why this note exists
Hockey operations added two new jobs mid-season: assistant scout and video analyst. This note is the contract later steps must follow. It does not rebuild staff sign-in or the existing coaching/scouting/medical access rules.

## Jobs already in the app (do not rebuild)
- Coaching: already built. Plans practices and games; does not see scouting notes or medical/sports-science data.
- Scouting: already built. Records and reviews player evaluations and competitive intelligence.
- Medical: already built. Tracks sports-science and medical-related information for player care.
- Out of scope: staff sign-in, the checks that prove who is logged in, and the existing coaching/scouting/medical access matrix.

## New jobs

### Assistant scout
- Who they are: supports the scouting team by recording and reviewing scouting observations under a scout's direction.
- Scouting information they need: can view and log scouting notes/events for players and games, the same kind of information full scouting staff work with.
- Game information they need: can view game schedules and results to know which games to scout.
- What they must never see: medical data, sports-science data, and medical flags, even on screens they're otherwise allowed to open.

### Video analyst
- Who they are: reviews game footage and in-game events to support coaching and scouting preparation.
- Scouting information they need: can view existing scouting notes for context when reviewing footage, but does not create or edit scouting notes.
- Game information they need: can view game schedules, results, and in-game events in detail, since this is their primary working data.
- What they must never see: medical data, sports-science data, and medical flags, even on screens they're otherwise allowed to open.

## What stays sealed
- Medical data: sealed from both the assistant scout and the video analyst. Neither job may view or receive medical records.
- Sports-science data: sealed from both the assistant scout and the video analyst. Neither job may view or receive sports-science/performance measures.
- Medical flags on shared screens: a shared screen (one more than one job can open) must not display a medical flag, such as "injured" or "not cleared to play," to the assistant scout or the video analyst, even if the rest of that screen is otherwise allowed for their job.

## Out of scope
- Rebuilding staff sign-in or the login/session checks.
- Changing the existing coaching, scouting, and medical access matrix.
- Any database changes, tests, or edits to the live website in this step — this note is a plain-language request only.

## What done means
- [ ] A teammate can tell what each new job may see without guessing.
- [ ] A teammate can tell that medical data, sports-science data, and medical flags on shared screens stay sealed.
- [ ] Nothing in this note asks to rebuild sign-in or the old access matrix.
