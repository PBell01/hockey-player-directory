# What You Can See

Welcome to hockey operations! Depending on your job, you will have access to different information in the app. This guide explains what you can see and what information is kept private.

## If You Are an Assistant Scout

**You can see:**
- Scouting notes and observations about players and games.
- Game schedules, results, and game information needed for scouting.
- You can also add new scouting notes and observations.

**You cannot see:**
- Medical records, health information, or injury details.
- Sports-science information, such as body measurements or training data.
- You cannot change existing scouting notes or add or change game information.

**On shared scouting or game screens:** Health and injury notes will not appear for you, even when you can see the other information on that screen.

## If You Are a Video Analyst

**You can see:**
- Existing scouting notes to help you understand players and game situations.
- Game schedules, results, and detailed in-game events to support your video reviews.

**You cannot see:**
- Medical records, health information, or injury details.
- Sports-science information, including body measurements and training data.
- You cannot add or change scouting notes or game information.

**On shared scouting or game screens:** Health and injury notes will not appear for you, even when you can see the other information on that screen.

## If You Already Work Here

### Coaches

**You can see:**
- Game schedules, results, and other game information.
- You can also add game information as part of your coaching responsibilities.

**You cannot see:**
- Scouting notes and player evaluations.
- Medical records, injury information, or sports-science data.
- Medical or injury notes on shared scouting screens.

### Scouts

**You can see:**
- Scouting notes and player evaluations.
- You can add new scouting notes and update existing ones.
- Game schedules, results, and other game information.

**You cannot see:**
- Medical records, health information, or injury details.
- Sports-science information, including training and body measurements.
- Medical or injury notes on shared scouting screens.
- You cannot add or change game information.

### Medical and Sports-Science Staff

**You can see:**
- Medical records and health information needed for player care.
- Sports-science information, including training and body measurements.
- You can add and update medical and sports-science records as part of your responsibilities.

**You cannot see:**
- Scouting notes or player evaluations.
- Game information used for coaching and scouting preparation.
- Medical flags on scouting-specific shared screens.

## If You Are Not Sure

If you are unsure whether you should have access to certain information, ask your hockey operations lead before proceeding. Only use information that is available to you for your assigned job.
